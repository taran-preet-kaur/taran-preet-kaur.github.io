Case study 01Paid media analysis

A2A | Google Ads performance analysis

Challenge

Understand why lead volume changed and whether paid advertising activity was contributing to the decline.

Approach

  • Compared the same 1–23 day window across September and October.
  • Reviewed spend, conversion volume and conversion value together rather than judging performance on leads alone.
  • Connected the timeline with the reported reduction/pausing of paid activity and identified the need to separate paid and organic lead sources.

Reported figures / deliverables

Google Ads spendA$983.90 → A$689.90
Conversion valueA$4,599.62 → A$4,519.23
Conversions51.99 → 47.97
ROAS4.67x → 6.55x

Context: October delivered higher reported ROAS with lower spend, while conversion volume and conversion value were slightly lower. This is an analysis of the supplied account figures, not a claim that one isolated change caused the movement.

Case study 02E-commerce paid media

Zenith Pharmacy | Shopping & Performance Max

Challenge

Monitor e-commerce campaign performance while working through Merchant Center and product-eligibility limitations.

Approach

  • Reviewed Shopping and Performance Max performance, conversion actions and product-feed constraints.
  • Tracked account-level spend, conversion value and reported conversions.
  • Flagged product limitations affecting items including Finacea and Phisohex, and reviewed the role of Search alongside Shopping/PMax.
  • Checked that purchase activity was treated as the primary e-commerce outcome while other shopping events remained secondary where appropriate.

Reported figures / deliverables

Reported spendA$129,475.96
Reported conversion valueA$318,458.03
Reported conversions1,812.81
Calculated ROAS2.46x

Context: These are the account totals shared in the working notes; the reporting date range was not supplied. ROAS is calculated as conversion value divided by spend.
